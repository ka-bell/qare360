# Qare 2.0 — Founding Documents

This folder is the source of truth for Qare 2.0.

These documents define why Qare exists and how that strategy becomes the website. Wireframes, copy, the AI Research Planner flow, and development should all follow from this set.

## Read order

1. **[Business Vision](01-business-vision.md)** — Why Qare exists: mission, problem, differentiation, Qare 360 model, and future direction.
2. **[UX Brief](02-ux-brief.md)** — How that vision translates into the website: goals, journey, IA, AI Research Planner, tone, and design principles.
3. **[Phase 2](03-phase-2.md)** — Homepage V1.0 is locked; remaining work is refinement, not redesign.

## Roles

| Document | Answers | Audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business Vision | Why do we exist? What do we believe? What model do we sell? | Founders, strategy, team |
| UX Brief | How does a visitor experience that on the website? | UX, design, copy, product, engineering |

Every major UX choice should trace back to the Business Vision. If a design decision cannot be linked to strategy, it does not belong.
