# Qare 2.0 — UX Brief

**Purpose of this document:** Translate the Business Vision into website goals, structure, flows and principles.

**Audience:** UX, design, copy, product and engineering.

**Related document:** [Business Vision](01-business-vision.md) — the strategy every choice below must support.

---

## Project Goal

**Strategic link:** Make professional research feel accessible — the visitor should experience Qare, not merely read about it.

Transform the current Qare website into a modern sales and onboarding platform.

The website should generate qualified leads while helping visitors discover the right research approach.

Visitors should leave feeling like they have already spoken to a research consultant.

---

## UX Principles

**Strategic link:** Remove complexity and guide organisations toward better decisions — not toward longer explanations.

The website should:

- Guide instead of explain
- Ask questions instead of showing long text
- Give value before asking for contact details
- Feel approachable
- Remove complexity
- Build trust

---

## Primary User

**Strategic link:** Serve organisations that value research but lack a dedicated research department — the gap between agencies and DIY tools.

- Marketing managers
- Brand managers
- Product managers
- Innovation teams
- Scale-ups
- SMEs

Companies that value research but don't have a dedicated research department.

---

## Website Goals

**Strategic link:** Convert interest into conversations that help organisations make better decisions — not into form submissions for their own sake.

- Generate more qualified leads
- Reduce friction
- Increase trust
- Demonstrate expertise
- Educate visitors
- Convert visitors into conversations

---

## Core User Journey

**Strategic link:** Help visitors ask the right questions before talking to a human — value first, contact second.

```
Visitor arrives
        ↓
"I have a business question."
        ↓
Qare helps identify the right research
        ↓
Visitor receives free strategic advice
        ↓
Visitor books an introduction
```

**Not:** Visitor fills in a contact form.

---

**Strategic link:** Structure the site around the Qare 360 lifecycle and decision-making — not around methodologies or internal service lists.

### Homepage

#### Hero

**Primary CTA**

**Secondary CTA**

## AI Research Planner

**Strategic link:** Become the easiest way to start research — a digital research consultant that reduces friction before a human conversation.

This is the centrepiece of the entire website.

It is not a chatbot.

It is not customer support.

It is a digital research consultant.

### Goal

Help organisations discover the right research before talking to a human.

### Example Flow

```
"What would you like to understand?"
        ↓
Tell us about your challenge
        ↓
AI asks intelligent follow-up questions
        ↓
AI Recommendation
        ↓
Book an introduction
```

**Follow-up question examples:**

- What decision are you trying to make?
- Have you already launched?
- Who is your audience?
- Which countries?
- Timeline?
- Budget?
- Existing research?
- Existing questionnaire?

### Recommendation Output

- Recommended research
- Timeline
- Estimated budget
- Suggested methodology
- Why this recommendation

**CTA:** Book an introduction.

### Future Vision

The planner becomes smarter over time.

Future capabilities could include:

- Reviewing uploaded questionnaires
- Reviewing campaign concepts
- Suggesting research methodologies
- Estimating sample sizes
- Reviewing discussion guides
- Generating research briefs
- Generating proposals
- Creating project timelines

The planner evolves into Qare's digital research consultant.
