# Qare 2.0 — Business Vision

**Purpose of this document:** Define why Qare exists, what problem we solve, how we are different, and the model everything fits into.

**Audience:** Founders, strategy, and the wider team.

**Related document:** [UX Brief](02-ux-brief.md) — how this vision translates into the website.

---

## Our Mission

Professional market research should not be reserved for organisations with enterprise budgets or dedicated research departments.

For decades, high-quality research has been expensive, time-consuming and difficult to access.

We believe every organisation deserves the confidence of professional research.

By combining experienced researchers, strategic thinking, AI and modern technology, Qare makes professional research more accessible without compromising on quality.

---

## Our Vision

We don't want to become another research agency.

We want to become the easiest way for organisations to make evidence-based decisions.

Just as Canva made professional design accessible…

Just as Stripe simplified online payments…

Qare wants to make professional research accessible for every organisation.

---

## Our Philosophy

Research is not the goal.

Better decisions are.

Research is simply the most effective way to reduce uncertainty before making important business decisions.

Our job is to make that process as accessible as possible.

---

## The Problem

Today organisations face two extremes.

### Traditional Research Agencies

- High budgets
- Long lead times
- Complex proposals
- Heavy reports
- Enterprise focused

### DIY Survey Tools

- Cheap
- Fast
- No strategy
- No expertise
- High risk of poor research

There is almost no solution in between.

---

## The Opportunity

Most organisations don't need a €100,000 research programme.

They need:

- Confidence
- Guidance
- Professional advice
- Reliable insights

They need someone who helps them ask the right questions.

## What Makes Qare Different

Qare combines four disciplines.

### Research

Experienced researchers and proven methodologies.

### Strategy

Understanding the business question before choosing the research method.

### Technology

Building smarter research experiences, dashboards and platforms.

### AI

Using AI to reduce friction, automate repetitive work and improve accessibility.

Technology is never the product.

Technology enables better research.

We don't sell surveys.

We help organisations make better decisions.

## The Qare 360 Model

Everything fits within one lifecycle.

### Validate

Before making a decision.

Questions like:

- Is this concept strong enough?
- Which message performs best?
- Which audience should we target?
- Should we launch?

### Measure

After making a decision.

Questions like:

- Did the campaign work?
- What changed?
- Did awareness increase?
- Are customers satisfied?

### Intelligence

When research becomes continuous.

Research evolves into:

- Dashboards
- Live reporting
- Research platforms
- AI Insights
- Continuous measurement

**Important:** Intelligence is intentionally not the main sales proposition. It is the natural next step after organisations become recurring research clients.
